- plan-scope: the deliverable count no longer makes a track `architecture` (`DELIVERABLE_LIMIT` and `deliverable_rule` are gone). A deliverable with task_class `03_refactoring` added to or retargeted onto a `light` track, or a `03_refactoring` build dispatch at the door, raises it to `architecture` and seeds `OI-PLAN-<track>` (rule `task_class 03_refactoring`). Nothing goes down by itself: tracks raised by the old count rule stay `architecture` until the operator lowers them.
