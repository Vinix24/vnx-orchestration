- The steering proposal store now knows the three permission kinds and the notification (ADR-084, permissieblokkades-leren L5c). A rejected `permissie`, `bouwer_allowlist` or `bouwgewoonte` proposal reopens only on `reopen_with_note` or a changed `handelingsklasse` (required, `K1`..`K8`, for `permissie`), never on fresh evidence.
