  operator decision 2026-10-17, ADR-008 amendment 2026-10-23): at `low` and `medium` only the first
  seat of the review stack is required, at `high` and `critical` every seat, each with a PASS on the

  `origin/main` before it reads the diff (`review_risk_class.merge_head_risk_class`); a failed fetch,
  an unreadable diff and a checkout that cannot be established are `high`. No flag lowers the class.
