- CI (deterministische-testverificatie PR-3): a new `red-on-base` signal job in `.github/workflows/vnx-ci.yml` turns the claim lines of the PR description (`**Red run** on <sha>:`, `Rode run:`, `Before the fix:` and the `RED_RUN_LABELS` the report contract defines) into `red_on_base.py --claims` input through `scripts/ci/red_run_claims.py`, then runs those tests on the merge-base and on the head. It runs on `pull_request` including `edited` and every pre-existing job excludes that action, so editing a PR body can never re-run the heavy jobs. A `no_test` or unclaimed `green_on_base` verdict stays a neutral mark; only a `fails_on_head`, `incomplete` or false claim turns the job red. Not a required check yet.
