- The cost-ledger replay keeps a record that a torn append would have lost (dispatch 20261020-kp-k2a1-r2, round 2 on #2141, ADR-043).
  Every append, in `cost_ledger.record_run` and in the replay, first ends a torn last line with `\n` under the ledger lock.
  No byte is removed: the torn prefix stays and counts as one unreadable line, and the next record starts on its own line.
  The replay reads the ledger back after its fsync and removes a spool file only when every record in it stands there as
  its own line, equal to what was spooled, with the newest record of each run as that run's last line. A file that does
  not read back stays in the spool, the `cost-ledger` beacon stays degraded and `vnx costs replay` exits 2
  (`CostLedgerReplayError`).
