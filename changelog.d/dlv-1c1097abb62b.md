- Active readers decide a `v3` dispatch on its ledger attempt (track `audit-spoor-kern`, K-7, ADR-045 §7): the new `scripts/lib/ledger_attempt_state.py` holds one `attempt_verdict(state_dir, project_id, dispatch_id, *, records=None)` over `ledger_end_line.end_line` and `dispatch_lifecycle.liveness`, and both readers now ask it instead of re-deriving "done" from receipts and age. `check_active_drain.py` moves an ended `completed` attempt to `completed/`, leaves any other end state as an open point with that end state, leaves a `live`/`unknown` attempt alone whatever its age, and books a `dead_no_end` attempt as `abandoned_candidate` (the K-6 sweep writes the end; the drain never writes ledger lines). The ledger is read once per drain run; an unreadable one makes every entry an open point, never a silent move.
- `scripts/lib/open_outcomes.py` takes the same verdict: a `v3` end line decides the item's outcome (`completed` closes it, `abandoned`/`timed_out`/`failed` stay listed until a T0 decision in `t0_decision_log.jsonl`), an end line without a readable `end_state` is a `malformed_end_line` open point, and a live/unknown attempt is no open point. Legacy dispatches (no `dispatch_started` line with `lifecycle_mode: v3`) keep the receipt-based path byte for byte.
- Tests: new `tests/test_k7_active_readers.py` drives real `dispatch_start.begin`, `dispatch_exit.build_end_line` and `dispatch_lifecycle.finalize` against a real ledger and `runtime_coordination.db` in `tmp_path` (only clocks and liveness probes are patched), covering both plan cases, every end state, T0 accept/reject, live/unknown past the threshold, the legacy reasons, an unreadable ledger, and two projects sharing a dispatch id (ADR-007).
- `open_outcomes` asks the attempt verdict before its legacy filter (K-7 round 2): a `v3` end state other than `completed` (`failed`, `timed_out`, `abandoned`, a malformed end) stays an open point until a T0 `accept`/`reject` in `t0_decision_log.jsonl`, even when the receipts fold to `superseded` (a child naming it as `parent_dispatch`) or to `accept`. Such an item carries `superseded_by` with the child ids, read from `summarize`'s single ledger pass. A legacy `superseded` dispatch is still no open point.
