- `scripts/events_archive_compress.py --project-id <pid> [--days N] [--apply] [--json]` compresses the old plain event archives of one store (the `events/archive` directory and its terminal subdirectories). Without `--apply` it is a dry-run that lists what a round would touch and changes nothing; `--days` defaults to 14 (ADR-081). Exit codes: 0 clean, 1 other failure, 2 usage or resolution error, 3 a divergent pair (never removed) (eventarchief-compressie C2b).
