- `scripts/daily_metrics.py` discovers its commands: every module in `scripts/lib/daily_metrics/commands/` with a callable `register(subparsers)` and `run(args)` becomes a subcommand, `daily` runs each module with an integer `DAILY_STEP` in `(DAILY_STEP, module name)` order, and a command that fails to import is reported on stderr as `{"command_import_failed": ...}` and makes `daily` exit 1 instead of being skipped (doorloop-en-effectiviteit-dagelijks PR-3c).
