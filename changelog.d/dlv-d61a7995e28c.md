- Cost ledger (track `kosten-per-model-in-het-grootboek`, K6, ADR-043): `vnx costs backfill` reads the deepseek-harness and glm-harness session transcripts from `--since` on and books one line per run. A transcript whose dispatch already has a live line becomes a correction on that `run_id` (`supersedes`, `backfill: true`); a transcript with no live line becomes a charge under the `backfill` producer. Tokens are deduplicated per `message.id`, split per peak/off-peak segment, and priced through the one ledger price function; provider and model come from the lane and the registry. A second `--apply` writes nothing, `--dry-run` is the default, and no message content leaves a transcript. The ledger now also accepts `unknown:<reason>` as a role, so a run without a dispatch row is booked with the reason it has.
