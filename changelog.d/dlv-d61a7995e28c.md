- Cost ledger (track `kosten-per-model-in-het-grootboek`, K6, ADR-043): `vnx costs backfill` reads the deepseek-harness and glm-harness session transcripts from `--since` on and books one line per run. A transcript whose dispatch already has a live line becomes a correction on that `run_id` (`supersedes`, `backfill: true`); a transcript with no live line becomes a charge under the `backfill` producer. Tokens are deduplicated per `message.id`, split per peak/off-peak segment, and priced through the one ledger price function; provider and model come from the lane and the registry. A second `--apply` writes nothing, `--dry-run` is the default, and no message content leaves a transcript. The ledger now also accepts `unknown:<reason>` as a role, so a run without a dispatch row is booked with the reason it has.
- Cost ledger backfill numbering: the plan reserves every attempt number it hands out, so two unbooked sessions of one dispatch book `backfill:<dispatch>:1` and `:2` (after any live line's number) instead of collapsing into one run; the numbering follows the run's end timestamp and then its transcript path, so a re-run plans the same ids.
- Cost ledger backfill identity (OI-2419): every booked line stores the transcript identity it was read from (`<lane>:<session id>`), and an existing run is only corrected on a verified identity match — a later session of the same dispatch, or a `--since` that leaves the booked transcript out of the scan, books its own attempt beside it, so its spend is added and never removed. Transcripts reached through overlapping or repeated `--harness-root` arguments (a parent dir and its lane dir, two stores holding one session) are deduplicated on the resolved path and on provider/session identity before planning, so one session is one run.
