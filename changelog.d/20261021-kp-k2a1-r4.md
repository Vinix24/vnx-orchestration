- The cost ledger's aggregation no longer returns a lower total marked complete while a replay runs (dispatch 20261021-kp-k2a1-r4, round 4 on #2141, ADR-043).
  `aggregate_runs` reads the ledger and counts the spool backlog in one lock scope, the ledger `flock` and then the spool
  lock, so a replay can no longer move a spooled record into the ledger between the two reads. With $1 in the ledger and
  $2 spooled it used to return `cost_usd=1`, `spool_pending=0` and no `incomplete` line. Without a ledger file the spool
  is counted first and the ledger checked after, and a ledger that a replay created in between is read under both locks.
