- Store path parity (track `vnx-init-store-path-parity`, PR-3): `vnx migrate --relocate-legacy --legacy-path <dir> --project-id <pid> [--assume-owner "<reason>"]` copies a pre-#1437 XDG store into the central `~/.vnx-data/<pid>` store and deletes nothing. Before writing it refuses an ambient `VNX_DATA_DIR`/`VNX_STATE_DIR` (or a mismatched `VNX_PROJECT_ID`), a repo-local store that would shadow the target, an existing target, a source with no database, and a missing `--legacy-path`/`--project-id` (no auto-discovery). Ownership comes from the `project_id` stamps in the source DBs: a foreign stamp refuses, no stamp at all (the schema-v14 shape) needs `--assume-owner "<reason>"`, and the assumption is recorded in `state/relocate-legacy.json` inside the published store. The copy stages into `~/.vnx-data/.staging-<pid>-<ts>/<pid>/` (SQLite DBs through the backup API, other files plain copies), runs `_bootstrap_runtime_dbs` plus the whole future-system pipeline there with every step fatal, requires `PRAGMA integrity_check` = `ok` on every DB, and publishes with one `os.rename`; a failure leaves the staging dir in place (path printed) and no target. The source is opened read-only and never modified — the removal command is printed as text for the operator. A store that holds a symlink anywhere in its tree (the store root itself may be a link) is refused before anything is read: `copytree` follows a linked directory while the database walk does not, so its WAL frames would be dropped from the copy.
