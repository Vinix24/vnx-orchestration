- Worktree-opruiming (track `worktree-opruiming-structureel`, PR-4): the daily sweep now reads one inventory of every fabric-owned worktree, locked or not, instead of only locked `dispatch-*` trees. Before anything is released a liveness guard must clear it — no held occupancy lock and no live process group in the dispatch process registry — and an unmeasurable probe keeps the tree. Unlocked trees are released only when clean, with nothing unpushed and no OPEN pull request; a detached `gate-*` tree qualifies when its HEAD is on some `origin/*` ref. `pool-*` and bench trees are listed but never released.
