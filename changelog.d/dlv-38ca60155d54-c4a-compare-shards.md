- `scripts/ci/check_xdist_parity.py compare` takes `--shard-dir` (once per Profile A shard, in order) as an alternative to `--candidate`, so the serial truth can be compared with the four shards of one run: the candidate is shard 1's serial phase plus every shard's own phase, read by the completeness check's shard reader, and a partition entry names the phases a test is in (`shard 2/4`, `serial`). The verdict records `candidate_shape` (`two_phase` or `shards`, with `shards`: N); a missing shard directory, plan or manifest, or a shard 1 without its serial phase is invalid evidence (exit 2). The two-phase verdict is unchanged apart from `candidate_shape` (pr-ci-onder-zes-minuten C4a).
