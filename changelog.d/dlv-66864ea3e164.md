- New `scripts/lib/event_archive_compress.py`: `compress_archive_round` compresses plain event-archive files older than N days to `<id>.ndjson.gz` losslessly (verified byte for byte), oldest first, holding only `.archive.lock` for the stat, replace and unlink; it settles plain-plus-gz pairs (equal, prefix, divergent) and stops an automatic round at 2 GiB read, 10 minutes or 500 files (eventarchief-compressie C2a, ADR-081). Library only: no CLI or sweep yet.
