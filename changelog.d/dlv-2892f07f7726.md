- Audit trail (plan row 6, OI-2522): the lifecycle sweep now also runs at the door on every real fire and in every `build_t0_state`, each bounded at 2 seconds between items (`budget_seconds`, reported as `budget_exhausted`/`budget_left` in the heartbeat and the state). A sweep that raises or runs out of budget never refuses a fire or changes a build's exit code. The `dispatch_started` line now carries the staged spec's own `deadline_seconds` when it is a legal integer, and `deadline_for` reads it first — so a spec staged under `pending/<staging_id>/` with a staging id different from the dispatch id no longer falls back to 14400 seconds.
