- voortgang mod (`packages/claude-code-toolkit/voortgang/`): a Claude Code mod that shows VNX progress per project in a pane (`/voortgang`) and a status line: live work, open outcomes, open PRs with their review seats, now-tracks with their deliverables and the deliverables waiting on a promote. It reads two files per project and runs two `vnx ... list --json --state-dir` calls, writes nothing, and shows a failed read as `onbeschikbaar` or `geen store`, never as zero.
