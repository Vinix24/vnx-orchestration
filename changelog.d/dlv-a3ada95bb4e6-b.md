- Permission events now become proposals (`permissie`, `bouwer_allowlist`, `bouwgewoonte`) once a form shows up in at least 3 sessions in 7 days, counted per form, origin, account, scope and audience. A hard stop, a wrapper or interpreter as the first word, a B4b event or a form that no rule can be built from stays a notification (`alleen_melding`). This is a pure computation in `scripts/lib/permission_proposals.py` and writes to no store. `permission_events.read_events` reads the ledger without writing to it, and skips and counts damaged lines, lines of another project and lines whose `event_id` does not match their fields (permissieblokkades-leren L5b). A wildcard rule (`prefix:*`) is a proposal only for a verb in the read-only table and only when no completion of its prefix reaches a hard stop; any other wildcard is a notification (`wildcard_not_read_only`), and `rg` is not in that table because `rg --pre` runs a command per file.
