- Cost ledger calibration (track `kosten-per-model-in-het-grootboek`, K5b, ADR-043 §7): a balance measurement is now its own record kind. `vnx costs observe` writes one `account_observation` per provider it can measure (deepseek: total, granted and topped-up balance; openrouter: total credits and total usage) to `events/provider_account_observations.ndjson`, appended atomically under its own lock file in the ledger's style (ADR-043 §6), with `unavailable:<reason>` when a probe fails — never a silent skip. `calibration_delta` reads the drop in the total balance as the spend of a window; a rise of the topped-up balance is a top-up, so a window that holds one says `unavailable:topup_in_window` instead of a number, and the windows around it are unaffected. `vnx costs report --by provider --json` carries that delta next to the ledger's metered sum with the deviation in percent. `openrouter_coverage` reports a generation without a ledger line as a missed catch when the API can list generations, and otherwise sets the `total_usage` delta against the ledger's OpenRouter sum and says that generation-level detection has no source. No spend or token sum reads the observations file.
