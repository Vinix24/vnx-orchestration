- A failed cost-ledger append no longer lets an older correction revert a newer one (dispatch 20261021-kp-k2a1-r5, round 5 on #2141, ADR-043).
  One lock orders a run's records: the spool lock. Every append, queue or spool decision and every spool write is made
  under it, inside the ledger `flock`; a failed append spools its record before either lock is released, and a writer
  that cannot open or lock the ledger takes the spool lock alone. Before, the fallback spool ran after the ledger lock
  was gone: a newer correction could land first and the replay put the older one after it (`cost_usd=1` instead of 2,
  `spool_pending=0`). The ledger lock is now released by closing the file, so bytes a failed write left buffered land
  under it, and the health beacon counts the spool under the spool lock.
