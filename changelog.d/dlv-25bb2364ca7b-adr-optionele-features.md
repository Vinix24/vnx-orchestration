- ADR-092 records the contract for optional features (optionele-features-expliciet ADR): a feature is on and fails closed, or is named off, and absence never decides. Every waived guarantee goes on one list, `waived_guarantees`, built once by `optional_features.waived_guarantees(project_id)` and carried on the dispatch plan, the `pr_merged` receipt, the `vnx-gate/door` check-run and `vnx doctor`. The CI requirement becomes one rule for request, verification, availability and the door (`gate_profile.ci_requirement`, extending ADR-060 § 5); branch protection separates declared from verified per `enforcement` mode; an unknown or unreadable plan-gate mode, and unreadable or unsupported plan-gate evidence under `required`, now refuse (superseding three rules of ADR-030); `hash_chain` joins the table with default off.
